How it works

Your ideas, better structured

Start with a topic, provide your own material, or combine both. CoursePilot will use the selected format and learning level to guide the result.

A few things to know

  • Your source material can guide the content.
  • Web research can supplement your material when enabled.
  • The learner level helps determine the depth of explanation.
  • You will be able to review the generated result before using it.
